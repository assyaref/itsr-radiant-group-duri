# ITSR Radiant Group Duri — User Manual

ITSR is how Duri branch staff report IT problems and follow them until they are fixed. It works in any browser on a computer or a phone.

## Signing in
1. Open the ITSR address your administrator gave you.
2. Enter your **employee number** (for example `EMP001`) and your **password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. After 5 wrong passwords your account is locked for 15 minutes. Forgot your password? Ask your administrator to reset it.
4. To sign out, use **Sign out** in the menu. On a phone, tap ☰ first.

## For all employees
**Dashboard.** Shows how many of your tickets are open, in progress and resolved, plus recent updates. Tap a number to see the list.

**Create a ticket.** Go to **New Ticket**, enter:
- *Subject*: a short summary ("Laptop does not turn on").
- *Category* and *Priority*. Use **Critical** only when work has stopped completely.
- *Description*: what happened, when it started, any error message, and the device or location.

Press **Submit ticket**. You get a ticket number (e.g. `ITSR-00012`) and IT Support is emailed.

**Follow your tickets.** Go to **My Tickets**. You can search and filter by status or priority. Open a ticket to see its status, the technician assigned, every change made, and the comments. You get an email when the status changes or IT replies. The browser address of a ticket page can be bookmarked or shared with IT. Only people allowed to see the ticket can open it.

**Comment.** Add more details or answer IT's questions in **Add a comment**. When the status is **Waiting for User**, IT needs a reply from you.

**When the ticket is resolved:**
- If the problem is fixed, press **Yes, close ticket**.
- If not, press **Reopen** and explain what is still wrong.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Waiting for IT Support |
| Assigned | A technician has taken it |
| In Progress | Work has started |
| Waiting for User | IT needs information from you |
| Resolved | IT has fixed it; please confirm |
| Closed | Finished |
| Reopened | Sent back to IT |

## For IT Support
- **Support Queue** shows every ticket. Filter **Assignee → Unassigned** to find new work.
- On a ticket: **Accept ticket** assigns it to you. **Assign to…** gives it to a colleague.
- Status buttons only show the steps that are allowed next. **Resolve** asks for a resolution; the employee sees it, so write it clearly. **Return to queue** releases the ticket.
- Comments: tick **Internal work note** for technical notes that only IT and administrators can see. Internal notes do not send email.
- You can change the priority at any time. Every change is recorded in History with your name.

## For Administrators
- **Users**: add employees and IT staff (employee number, name, email, department, role, initial password). You can also edit details or roles, reset passwords, and deactivate people who leave. Deactivated users lose access immediately; their tickets are kept. You cannot remove your own administrator role, and at least one administrator must remain.
- **Categories**: add, rename or deactivate categories. Categories are never deleted, so existing tickets keep them.
- **Settings**: the IT Support group email and manager email are CC'd on notifications. Also set the application URL used for links in emails, and turn email on or off.
- Administrators can also do everything IT Support can.

## Accessibility
ITSR exposes its navigation, ticket text, comments and buttons to assistive technology. In the browser, screen-reader users first activate the hidden **Enable accessibility** button, which is the first item reached with the Tab key. This has been verified through the browser's accessibility tree; testing with specific screen-reader products (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack) has not been done yet.

## Help
If a page shows an error, press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, contact IT Support and quote the ticket number.
